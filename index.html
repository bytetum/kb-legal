bytetum kb

bytetum kb is a personal knowledge base, run by its owner for their own use. Its calendar sync connects the owner's kb to the owner's own Google Calendar, so that routines and events kept in the kb appear in a calendar the app creates in that account.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
